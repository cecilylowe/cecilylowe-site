Ceramics

We are here once, briefly, between dust and dust. The beautiful part is the in-between: a turn to create and to do what is meaningful.

Nothing I create is finished. I make ceramics to bring objects into the world long enough to produce a feeling or a shift in attention. The form is then broken down, the clay reclaimed, and reintroduced again. The object is not an endpoint but an occupant of the in-between, taking its turn to be beautiful between dust and dust. As I take mine.
